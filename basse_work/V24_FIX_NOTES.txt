BASSE ONLINE SHOP V24

1. GPS/map fix: use the actual The Gambia country polygon instead of a loose rectangle.
2. Invalid/stale customer and driver GPS coordinates are cleared on startup, restore, and tracking responses.

5. Driver GPS client uses the same tighter Gambia geographic sanity bounds before sending.
6. Maps remain standard Leaflet + OpenStreetMap raster tiles with the normal Leaflet attribution: "Leaflet | © OpenStreetMap contributors". No API key.
